Roof Replacement in Bakersfield: What the Sun Has Already Decided

A Bakersfield shingle roof lives a hard life. The normal July high sits near 98 degrees, the record is 118, and from May to September the UV strips granules off south and west slopes years early.

Replacement here means tear-off to bare deck, decking repairs priced per sheet, synthetic underlayment, new flashings and pipe boots, and a shingle chosen with the energy code in view, since the city sits in climate zone 13.

Re-roofing is permitted work through the City's Building Division, and we file it. If your roof is tile, the answer is often a lift-and-relay instead.

How a Job Runs

From Roof Walk to Final Walk in 4 Steps

The same order on every Bakersfield job, whether it is a pipe boot or a full tile relay.

1

Roof Walk and Attic Check

We walk every slope, photograph granule loss, flashing and vent condition, and look at the underside of the deck from the attic for staining and daylight.

2

Written Scope and Shingle Choice

You receive the photos, a square count, the underlayment and shingle options with their Title 24 standing, and a price before the permit is filed.

3

Permit and Tear-Off Day

The City or Kern County permit is pulled, material is delivered, and the crew strips the old roof, repairs decking and dries the house in.

4

Final Walk and Sign-Off

New shingles, flashings and vents are checked slope by slope with you, the attic is checked for airflow, and the permit inspection is closed out.

Common Questions

Roof Replacement — Frequently Asked Questions

What does a full roof replacement cost per square foot in California?
The California market tends to run about $7.50 to $9.50 per square foot installed for architectural asphalt shingles, about $8.00 to $10.50 for cool-roof shingles and about $10.00 to $18.00 for concrete or clay tile. Per roofing square that is roughly $750 to $1,800 by material. Steep pitches, two tear-off layers and decking repairs push a Bakersfield job toward the top of those bands.
Should I repair or replace a sun-damaged roof in Bakersfield?
Replace when the damage is everywhere, repair when it is in one place. Granules gone from most south and west slopes, curled edges across the field and more than one leak mean the roof is finished; one failed pipe boot or a lifted flashing means years left. The roof walk photos show which case yours is, and our repair service handles the second.
How long does a Bakersfield roof replacement take?
Most shingle replacements run about 2 to 6 days depending on roof size and type, and summer crews start at daybreak because shingles soften in afternoon heat. Tear-off and dry-in are planned so the deck is covered before the crew leaves each day. Decking surprises or a second old layer can add a day, so the finish date is a plan that can move.
Will my new roof have to meet Title 24?
Probably. Bakersfield sits in Energy Commission climate zone 13, and California's Title 24 energy code sets cool-roof requirements for many roof replacements in the Central Valley; the permit paperwork is where that is checked. It means a shingle with a listed solar reflectance, and those come in gray, brown and charcoal. Our cool roof page covers the options.
What is the most expensive part of replacing a roof?
Labor and the shingle itself, in roughly that order, with tear-off and disposal behind them. On an older Bakersfield house the wild card is decking: plywood that baked under a slow leak for years can be soft in patches, and each replaced sheet adds material and time. We price decking per sheet on the written scope, and any added sheets are discussed with you before they go on.
